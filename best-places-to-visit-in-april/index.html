Best places to visit in India in April

Plan your April trip with weather-matched pilgrimage and holiday guides.

April heats up quickly in the plains, so plan sightseeing for early mornings. The hills open up, and the Char Dham shrines in Uttarakhand begin to reopen from late April.

Our guides rate 13 places as at their best in April: Darjeeling, Haridwar, Jibhi and Tirthan Valley homestays, Kamakhya, Kinnaur homestays: Sangla, Chitkul and Kalpa, Lava, Kolakham and Rishyap homestays, Parvati Valley homestays: Kasol, Tosh and Kalga, Rameswaram, Rishikesh, Sham Valley homestays: Alchi, Likir and Ule, Sandakphu and Singalila trail stays, Sittong homestays, Vaishno Devi.

Festivals this month

  • Badrinath: Kapat opening (April/May)

Less ideal in April

  • Badrinath: May-June and September-October. Monsoon landslides make July-August risky. Closed in winter (around November to April).
  • Kedarnath: May-June and mid-September to October. Avoid July-August monsoon, when landslides frequently block roads. The temple is closed from November to April.
  • Markha Valley trek homestays: June to September, when the passes are open and homestays operate.
  • Varanasi : October to March is pleasant. Summers (April-June) are very hot; during monsoon the river rises and some ghats and boat services close.
