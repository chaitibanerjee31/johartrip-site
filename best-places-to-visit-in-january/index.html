Best places to visit in India in January

Plan your January trip with weather-matched pilgrimage and holiday guides.

January is cold across North India, with fog that often delays trains and flights in the early morning. South India and the coast are at their most comfortable, and Makar Sankranti fairs begin the festival year.

Our guides rate 33 places as at their best in January: Alleppey Backwaters, Andaman Islands, Auli, Ayodhya, Coorg, Dooars homestays, Dwarka, Goa, Gokarna, Gulmarg, Hampi, Haridwar, Hornbill Festival, Kohima, Jaipur, Jaisalmer, Kamakhya, Kaziranga National Park, Kolkata, Manali, Meghalaya, Munnar, Pondicherry, Puri, Rameswaram, Rann of Kutch, Ranthambore National Park, Sittong homestays, Somnath, Sundarbans, Tirupati & Tirumala, Udaipur, Ujjain, Varanasi.

Festivals this month

  • Andaman Islands: Island Tourism Festival (January)
  • Dwarka: Janmashtami
  • Jaipur: Jaipur Literature Festival (Jan)
  • Pondicherry: Pongal (January)
  • Sundarbans: Bonbibi puja (January/February)

Less ideal in January

  • Badrinath: May-June and September-October. Monsoon landslides make July-August risky. Closed in winter (around November to April).
  • Hanle and Changthang homestays: May to October; winter is extremely cold and roads may be difficult.
  • Kedarnath: May-June and mid-September to October. Avoid July-August monsoon, when landslides frequently block roads. The temple is closed from November to April.
  • Kinnaur homestays: Sangla, Chitkul and Kalpa: April to June and September to November; winter has heavy snow on higher roads.
  • Markha Valley trek homestays: June to September, when the passes are open and homestays operate.
  • Nubra Valley and Turtuk homestays: June to September, when roads are open; spring and autumn can be cold and shoulder-season roads vary.
  • Sandakphu and Singalila trail stays: April to May and October to November for clear views. Winter is very cold with snow; monsoon is wet and views are poor.
  • Spiti Valley homestays: May to October for open roads. Winter is stark and cold but offers a unique experience for prepared travellers.
