Shakti Peetha Darshan

Powerful shrines of the Goddess where parts of Sati are believed to have fallen.

The Shakti Peethas are revered shrines of the Divine Mother. Lists vary from 51 to 108; the most visited include Kamakhya in Assam, Kalighat in Kolkata, Tarapith, Jwalamukhi and Naina Devi in Himachal, and Ambaji in Gujarat. Vaishno Devi is one of the most visited Devi shrines in India.

The route, stop by stop

  1. Kamakhya (Guwahati)Assam. Kamakhya temple on Nilachal hill in Guwahati is one of the most important Shakti Peethas and the centre of Tantric worship, famous for the Ambubachi Mela.
  2. KolkataWest Bengal. Kolkata pairs the Kalighat Shakti Peetha, Dakshineswar and Belur Math with colonial heritage, legendary food and UNESCO-listed Durga Puja.
  3. Vaishno Devi (Katra)Jammu & Kashmir. The holy cave of Mata Vaishno Devi on the Trikuta mountains is reached by a well-paved trek of about 12-13 km from Katra, with ponies, battery cars and helicopters for help.
