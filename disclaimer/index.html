Accuracy

JoharTrip publishes general travel and pilgrimage information. Temple timings, darshan and aarti schedules, ticket prices, registration rules, opening/closing dates, routes, distances and transport services change often and sometimes without notice. Distances and durations are approximate. Always confirm with the official temple trust, state tourism department, IRCTC, airline or operator before you travel. Each page shows the date it was last checked.

Health and safety

High-altitude shrines (such as Kedarnath, Badrinath, Yamunotri, Amarnath) and long treks can be dangerous, especially for elderly people, pregnant women, children and anyone with heart, lung, blood-pressure or other medical conditions. Consult a qualified doctor before undertaking such journeys. Follow weather warnings and orders of local administration and disaster-management authorities. Information here is not medical advice.
