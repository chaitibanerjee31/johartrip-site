Best places to visit in India in September

Plan your September trip with weather-matched pilgrimage and holiday guides.

September is when the monsoon withdraws and the countryside is green. The Himalayan shrines become accessible again, and festival season starts with Ganesh Chaturthi and the build-up to Durga Puja.

Our guides rate 13 places as at their best in September: Badrinath, Hanle and Changthang homestays, Jibhi and Tirthan Valley homestays, Kedarnath, Kinnaur homestays: Sangla, Chitkul and Kalpa, Markha Valley trek homestays, Nubra Valley and Turtuk homestays, Parvati Valley homestays: Kasol, Tosh and Kalga, Rishikesh, Sham Valley homestays: Alchi, Likir and Ule, Spiti Valley homestays, Tirupati & Tirumala, Vaishno Devi.

Festivals this month

  • Badrinath: Mata Murti ka Mela (Sept)
  • Tirupati & Tirumala: Srivari Brahmotsavam (Sept-Oct)

Less ideal in September

  • Dooars homestays: October to March for wildlife and mild weather. Summer is hot and humid; parks may close in the monsoon.
  • Goa: November to February. Monsoon (June-Sept) is lush and cheap but beaches are rough.
  • Lava, Kolakham and Rishyap homestays: March to May and October to December for clear mountain views. Monsoon (June to September) is green and quiet but has landslide risk and leeches.
  • Sandakphu and Singalila trail stays: April to May and October to November for clear views. Winter is very cold with snow; monsoon is wet and views are poor.
