Shrines of Vishnu, Krishna and Rama: Tirupati, Badrinath, Puri, Dwarka and Ayodhya.

Vishnu and Krishna shrines

Ayodhya

Uttar Pradesh

Birthplace of Lord Rama on the banks of the Saryu

Best: Oct to Mar

Badrinath

Uttarakhand

Lord Vishnu's abode between the Nar and Narayan ranges

Best: May to Jun, Sep to Oct

Dwarka

Gujarat

Krishna's golden kingdom on the Arabian Sea

Best: Oct to Mar

Puri

Odisha

Lord Jagannath's seaside dham and the world-famous Rath Yatra

Best: Oct to Mar

Tirupati & Tirumala

Andhra Pradesh

Darshan of Lord Venkateswara on the seven hills

Best: Sep to Feb

Best timeFestival seasonLess ideal

Highlights

  • Ayodhya: Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir
  • Badrinath: One of the four original Char Dham
  • Dwarka: Dwarkadhish temple and the daily flag ceremony
  • Puri: Darshan at Shree Jagannath temple
  • Tirupati & Tirumala: Darshan of Sri Venkateswara at Tirumala
