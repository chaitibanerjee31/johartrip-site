Content, corrections & takedown policy

Effective 2026-11-09

How we write

Guides are written in our own words from official websites, government tourism portals and openly licensed sources (credited on each page). Our automated checks reject text that copies too closely from any source. We do not copy content from other travel websites.

Corrections

Email contact@johartrip.com with subject “Correction”, the page link and the correct information (ideally with an official source). We aim to update within 7 working days and record the “last checked” date on the page.

Copyright and takedown requests

If you believe content on JoharTrip infringes your copyright or other rights, email contact@johartrip.com with subject “Takedown” and include: your name and contact details; the work you own; the exact URL on our site; a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised; and a statement that the information is accurate and you are the owner or authorised to act. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and, where the claim is valid, remove or disable access within 36 hours, consistent with the IT Rules, 2021.

Objectionable content

Requests relating to religious sentiment, privacy, defamation or safety are handled by our Grievance Officer at contact@johartrip.com (subject “Grievance”) within the same timelines.

Traveller stories

Stories are reviewed by a person before they appear. We do not publish stories that are abusive, discriminatory, defamatory, promotional, contain private details (phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers) of others, or make unproven accusations against named people or businesses. We show first names only. Authors can ask for removal at any time. Anyone can report a story using the link under it.

Customer reviews

Reviews must be honest first-hand experience. We refuse or hide reviews that contain links, phone numbers or email addresses, promotions, abuse, or accusations against named individuals. Paid, rewarded, self-written (by the business) or competitor reviews are not allowed. Anyone can report a review; several reports hide it until we check it.
