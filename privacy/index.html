Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-10

This policy explains how JoharTrip handles personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable rules. JoharTrip is the Data Fiduciary for data described here.

What we collect

  • Data you give us: email address (newsletter), name, email and message (contact form), and business details (advertisers).
  • Usage data: anonymous or pseudonymous page views, device and browser type, approximate region and clicks on links, collected by analytics tools.
  • Cookies: set by analytics, advertising (Google AdSense, if enabled) and affiliate partners when you click their links. See “Cookies” below.
  • Trip planner: your choices are stored only in your own browser and are not sent to us.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. Please do not submit personal data if you are under 18 without a parent's consent.

Why we use it (purpose)

  • To reply to you, send the newsletter you asked for, and manage advertising agreements.
  • To measure and improve the website and decide which guides to write.
  • To show ads and earn affiliate commissions that keep the site free.
  • To comply with law and prevent misuse.

We process data on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, or for legitimate uses permitted by law. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

With service providers who help us run the site (hosting, email/forms, analytics, advertising) only for these purposes; with affiliate partners when you click their links; and with authorities when required by law.

Cookies

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. If you decline, we do not load advertising cookies. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also clear cookies in your browser at any time.

Retention

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe; contact messages for up to 2 years; advertiser records as long as required for tax and accounting law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction, completion or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance. Write to contact@johartrip.com (subject “Privacy”). If unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we keep data to a minimum. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, JoharTrip — contact@johartrip.com. Acknowledgement within 24 hours; resolution within 15 days.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new effective date.

Reviews, stories and the trip assistant

When you post a review we store your first name, rating, text and a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address to limit spam; the address itself is not kept. Stories and requirement forms sent by email are used only to reply and, for stories you approve, to publish them. The trip assistant runs in your browser and keeps your answers only for that visit.

Get temple dates before you book

A short monthly email with opening dates, festivals and new guides. No spam.
