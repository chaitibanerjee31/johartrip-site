About Hampi

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire between the 14th and 16th centuries. Its ruins spread over more than 25 sq km. Virupaksha Temple is still active, while the Vittala Temple, with its stone chariot, is the best-known monument.

The place is best explored by bicycle or auto-rickshaw, in two zones: the royal centre around the Hazara Rama Temple and Lotus Mahal, and the sacred centre along the river. Sunrise at Matanga Hill and sunset at Hemakuta Hill are classic.

Across the river, Virupapura Gaddi and Anegundi have a laid-back feel, guesthouses and a short trek to Anjanadri Hill, believed to be Hanuman's birthplace. The Hampi Utsav festival is usually held in early November.

Highlights

  • Virupaksha Temple
  • Vittala Temple and stone chariot
  • Hemakuta and Matanga hills
  • Lotus Mahal and Elephant Stables
  • Anegundi and Anjanadri Hill

Things to do in Hampi

  1. Cycle the ruins

    Rent a bicycle and cover the royal centre in a day.

  2. Sunrise at Matanga Hill

    Climb before dawn for a wide view over the ruins.

  3. Vittala Temple

    See the stone chariot and musical pillars.

  4. Cross to Anegundi

    Visit by boat, then climb Anjanadri Hill.

  5. Boat or coracle

    Check local rules on river rides.

How to reach Hampi

By air

Hubballi (about 140 km) and Ballari/Toranagallu airports have limited flights; Bengaluru (BLR) is the main gateway.

By train

Hosapete (HPT) is about 13 km away with trains from Bengaluru, Hubballi and Hyderabad.

By road

About 340 km from Bengaluru. Overnight buses run from Bengaluru and Goa.

Getting around

Bicycles, scooters and autos.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Best time to visit

October to February is comfortable. March to May is very hot. Heavy monsoon rains arrive in June-September.

Month by month: January, February, October, November, December.

Festivals

Hampi Utsav (usually November, dates vary)Virupaksha Car Festival (Feb/Mar)

Travel tips

  • Start early; midday heat is intense even in winter.
  • Carry water, a hat and sun protection.
  • Some monuments have ASI entry fees; check at the gate.
Etiquette. Remove footwear and dress modestly at active temples.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hampi a pilgrimage place?

Yes, partly. Virupaksha Temple and Anjanadri are active sites, but most visitors come for heritage.
