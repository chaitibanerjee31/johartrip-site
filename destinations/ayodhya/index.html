About Ayodhya

Ayodhya, on the banks of the Saryu, is revered as the birthplace of Lord Rama and is one of the Sapta Puri. The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, consecrated in January 2024 and built in the Nagara style, has transformed the city into one of India's busiest pilgrimage destinations.

Traditionally, pilgrims first pay respects at Hanuman Garhi, a hilltop fort-temple, before going for Ram Lalla's darshan. Kanak Bhawan, gifted to Sita by Kaikeyi according to tradition, is known for its beautiful idols of Rama and Sita.

In the evenings, the Saryu aarti and the lights at Ram ki Paidi are magical. During Deepotsav, on the eve of Diwali, lakhs of diyas light the ghats.

The city is compact and easy to explore over a day or two, with new roads, parking areas and pilgrim facilities around the main temples.

Things to do in Ayodhya

  1. Ram Mandir darshan

    Phones, bags and electronics are not allowed; use free lockers. Aarti passes can be booked on the official Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra website.

  2. Hanuman Garhi

    Climb about 70 steps to the temple; tradition says visit before Ram Lalla.

  3. Saryu Aarti

    Held at Naya Ghat in the evening; boat rides on the Saryu are also available.

  4. Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal

    Two of the most important old temples in the city.

  5. Guptar Ghat

    A peaceful ghat about 10 km away, associated with Rama's departure from the world.

How to reach Ayodhya

By air

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, Ayodhya Dham (AYJ), about 15 km from the temple, has flights from major cities. Lucknow airport is about 140 km away.

By train

Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) has direct trains, including Vande Bharat, from Delhi and other cities. Ayodhya Cantt (formerly Faizabad) is nearby.

By road

About 135 km from Lucknow and 200 km from Varanasi; UPSRTC buses run frequently.

Getting around

E-rickshaws and electric buses connect the main sites.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Sapta Puri · Ram Janmabhoomi · Saryu

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Ayodhya is counted among the seven sacred cities in tradition and is the birthplace of Rama in the Ramayana.
  • Historical record The site was the subject of a long legal dispute decided by the Supreme Court of India in November 2019.
  • Historical record The Ram temple's consecration (Pran Pratishtha) took place on 22 January 2024.
  • Tradition Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan and the Saryu ghats are the other main stops.

Steps to follow

  1. Reach Ayodhya Dham Junction or the Maharishi Valmiki airport, or come from Lucknow (about 135 km) or Varanasi.
  2. Register and book darshan and aarti passes on the Trust's website; slots can be limited.
  3. Check what you may carry. Security is tight and phones are generally not allowed in.
  4. See Hanuman Garhi first by tradition, then Ram Mandir, Kanak Bhawan and the Saryu aarti in the evening.
