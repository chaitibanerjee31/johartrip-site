About Jaipur

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur was one of India's first planned cities. Its old walled city, painted terracotta pink, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Amber Fort on the hills, the honeycomb facade of Hawa Mahal, the City Palace and the Jantar Mantar observatory are the classic sights. Jaipur is part of the Delhi-Agra-Jaipur Golden Triangle.

For devotees, the Govind Dev Ji temple inside the City Palace complex is the heart of the city's Krishna worship, while Galtaji, Moti Dungri Ganesh and Birla Mandir are also popular.

The city is a good base for day trips to Amber, Sanganer's block-printing workshops, Khatu Shyam Ji and the Brahma temple at Pushkar. Evenings in the old city bazaars, with lit-up gateways and street food, are a highlight of any visit.

Things to do in Jaipur

  1. Amber Fort

    Go at opening time; the composite ticket covers several monuments.

  2. Govind Dev Ji aarti

    Seven jhanki darshans a day; the morning Mangla aarti is the most popular.

  3. Nahargarh at sunset

    Panoramic views over the city.

  4. Shopping

    Block-print textiles, blue pottery, jewellery and mojaris in the old city bazaars.

  5. Rajasthani food

    Dal baati churma, pyaaz kachori and lassi.

How to reach Jaipur

By air

Jaipur International Airport (JAI), about 12 km from the city.

By train

Jaipur Junction (JP) with Vande Bharat and Shatabdi trains from Delhi.

By road

About 280 km from Delhi via the expressway; frequent Volvo buses.

Getting around

Metro, autos and app cabs.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.
