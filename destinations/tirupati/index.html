About Tirupati & Tirumala

High on the seven hills of Tirumala, above the town of Tirupati, stands the temple of Lord Venkateswara, also called Srinivasa and Balaji. Tens of thousands of devotees visit every day, and the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) runs one of the most organised pilgrim systems in India, from queue complexes to free meals at the Annaprasadam hall.

Many devotees offer their hair at the Kalyanakatta as a sign of surrender, and almost everyone returns with the famous Tirupati laddu. Some climb to the temple on foot along the Alipiri or Srivari Mettu footpaths as a vow.

Tirupati itself has important temples too - Sri Padmavathi Ammavari temple at Tiruchanur, Govindaraja Swamy temple and Kapila Theertham waterfall shrine - which complete the pilgrimage.

Highlights

  • Darshan of Sri Venkateswara at Tirumala
  • Walking up via Alipiri or Srivari Mettu footpath

Things to do in Tirupati & Tirumala

  1. Book darshan online

    Special Entry Darshan (Rs 300) and accommodation are released in advance on the official TTD website. Free Sarva Darshan tokens are also issued; waiting can be long in peak periods.

  2. Climb the footpath

    Alipiri is the traditional route with thousands of steps; Srivari Mettu is shorter. Footpath walkers can get Divya Darshan tokens - check current rules on the TTD site.

  3. Padmavathi Ammavari temple

    Tradition says a visit to Goddess Padmavathi at Tiruchanur, about 5 km from Tirupati, should accompany Balaji darshan.

  4. Explore Tirumala

    Visit Akasa Ganga, Papavinasanam, Silathoranam (natural rock arch) and the Sri Venkateswara Museum.

  5. Kapila Theertham

    A Shiva temple at the foot of the hills with a waterfall, especially beautiful after the monsoon.

How to reach Tirupati & Tirumala

By air

Tirupati Airport (TIR) at Renigunta, about 15 km from Tirupati, has flights from Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai and other cities. Chennai airport is about 140 km away.

By train

Tirupati (TPTY) and Renigunta (RU) stations are connected to Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Kolkata and Delhi.

By road

Frequent APSRTC buses link Tirupati with Chennai (about 140 km), Bengaluru (about 250 km) and Hyderabad. TTD free buses and APSRTC buses run up the ghat road to Tirumala.

Getting around

Tirumala has free TTD buses connecting cottages, the temple and other sights.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Divya Desam · Vaishnava · Tirumala Venkateswara

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Tirumala is among the 108 Divya Desams in the Alvar hymns.
  • Official statement The temple is managed by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD), a statutory body of Andhra Pradesh.
  • Official statement The Tirupati laddu has been granted a Geographical Indication, a record that links it to this temple.
  • Tradition Many devotees offer their hair at Tirumala as a vow. Tradition ties it to surrendering ego.

Steps to follow

  1. Pick a darshan type on the TTD site: free Sarva Darshan, a paid Special Entry Darshan ticket, or other categories. Special Entry tickets are released online on set dates, so check the release calendar.
  2. Stay in Tirupati town (cheaper, more choice) or at Tirumala (TTD rooms book out fast and are released online).
  3. Go up by road (about 22 km from Tirupati) or climb the Alipiri footpath (about 3,500 steps). Walkers can often get a quicker queue token; confirm current TTD rules.
  4. Follow the dress code: traditional wear is expected (saree or half-saree, salwar with dupatta, or dhoti/kurta). Jeans and shorts may be refused.
  5. Plan a full day for darshan. Waiting can be 6 to 20 hours in the free queue on busy days.
  6. Nearby: Sri Padmavathi temple at Tiruchanur, Kapilatheertham, Srikalahasti (about 36 km) for a half-day add-on.

Make the trip count

  • Only the TTD portal sells official tickets. Brokers who offer 'quick darshan' are not the temple.
  • Brahmotsavam in September or October and weekend and holiday periods are the busiest.
  • Tonsure, rooms and darshan are separate bookings; confirm each.

Best time to visit

September to February is most comfortable. Brahmotsavam (Sept/Oct) and Vaikunta Ekadasi are spectacular but extremely crowded.

Travel tips

  • Book darshan and rooms on the official TTD website only - it opens bookings months ahead.
  • Traditional dress is required for darshan (dhoti/kurta-pyjama for men; saree/salwar with dupatta for women).
  • Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside; use the free deposit counters.
  • Carry your ID used during booking.
Etiquette. Strict dress code and no photography inside. Respect queue rules; touts selling 'quick darshan' are illegal.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book Tirupati darshan?

Through the official TTD website or app. Special Entry Darshan slots are released in advance and sell out fast.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, traditional Indian attire is mandatory for darshan.
