About Ujjain

Ujjain, one of the Sapta Puri, is ruled by Mahakal. The Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga is the only one of the twelve that faces south (Dakshinamukhi), and its pre-dawn Bhasma Aarti, in which the lingam is adorned with sacred ash, is one of the most powerful rituals in Hindu worship.

The Shri Mahakal Lok corridor, with statues and murals from Shiva's stories, has made the temple area beautiful and easy to visit. The city is also known for Kal Bhairav, where liquor is offered to the deity, Harsiddhi Shakti Peetha and Ram Ghat on the Shipra.

Ujjain hosts the Simhastha Kumbh Mela every 12 years; the next is due in 2028. It is often combined with Omkareshwar, another Jyotirlinga on the Narmada.

Things to do in Ujjain

  1. Bhasma Aarti

    Book in advance on the official Mahakaleshwar temple website. A dress code applies (dhoti for men, saree for women). Reporting time is very early, around 2-3 am.

  2. Mahakal Lok

    Walk the corridor in the evening when it is lit up.

  3. Kal Bhairav temple

    About 6 km away, one of the city's most unique temples.

  4. Harsiddhi and Ram Ghat

    See the lamp towers lit at Harsiddhi in the evening, then the aarti at Ram Ghat.

  5. Omkareshwar day trip

    About 140 km; the Jyotirlinga sits on an Om-shaped island in the Narmada.

How to reach Ujjain

By air

Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport, Indore (IDR), about 55 km away.

By train

Ujjain Junction (UJN) is connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Kolkata and other cities.

By road

About 55 km from Indore and 190 km from Bhopal; frequent buses.

Getting around

Autos and e-rickshaws; most temple areas are walkable.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Sapta Puri (Avantika) · Jyotirlinga (Mahakaleshwar) · Kumbh site

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Mahakaleshwar is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and is the only one facing south (Dakshinamukhi), according to temple tradition.
  • Tradition Ujjain is counted as Avantika among the seven sacred cities.
  • Tradition It hosts the Simhastha Kumbh on the Shipra river.
  • Tradition Sandipani Ashram is where tradition says Krishna studied.
  • Historical record Jantar Mantar observatory, built under Jai Singh II in the 18th century, is documented.

Steps to follow

  1. Decide if you want the Bhasma Aarti, held very early in the morning. It needs advance registration through the temple's system; check the temple's current process.
  2. Reach Ujjain by train; Indore airport is about 55 km away.
  3. Visit Kal Bhairav, Harsiddhi, Ram Ghat and Sandipani Ashram on day two.
  4. Pair with Omkareshwar and Maheshwar (about 140 km) for a two-day Malwa circuit.

Make the trip count

  • Beware of fake Bhasma Aarti 'tickets'. Use only the temple's own system.
  • Simhastha years bring very heavy demand for rooms.
