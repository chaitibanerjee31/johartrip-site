About Meghalaya (Shillong and Cherrapunji)

Shillong, the capital, has lakes, a good food scene and a lively music culture. The town is a base for trips to Cherrapunji (Sohra), Mawlynnong and Dawki.

Nongriat's double-decker living root bridge is a famous trek with thousands of steps. Dawki's Umngot river has clear water and boat rides. Mawphlang's sacred forest is a short drive from Shillong.

A cherry blossom festival is usually held in Shillong in November. Dates vary, so confirm with the tourism department.

Things to do in Meghalaya

  1. Trek to Nongriat

    Reach the double-decker bridge on foot.

  2. Boat at Dawki

    Check river conditions.

  3. Visit Mawlynnong

    A tidy village with a skywalk view.

  4. Explore caves

    Mawsmai Cave is easy.

  5. Eat Khasi food

    Try jadoh and doh khlieh.

How to reach Meghalaya

By air

Guwahati (GAU) is about 100 km from Shillong; Shillong Airport has limited flights.

By train

Guwahati is the main railhead.

By road

Guwahati to Shillong takes about 3-4 hours by shared taxi or bus.

Getting around

Taxis are the main way around.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Is it good in winter?

Yes; the skies are clearer, though waterfalls are thinner than in the monsoon.
