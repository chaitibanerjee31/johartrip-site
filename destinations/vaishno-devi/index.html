About Vaishno Devi (Katra)

Every year millions of devotees chant 'Jai Mata Di' as they climb to the holy cave of Mata Vaishno Devi, where the goddess is worshipped in the form of three natural rock formations (pindis) representing Maha Kali, Maha Lakshmi and Maha Saraswati. Devotees believe one reaches the shrine only when Mata calls.

The yatra begins at Katra, a busy town at the foot of the Trikuta hills. A wide, lit and paved path - with shelters, food stalls and medical points - climbs to the Bhawan. The route passes Banganga, Charan Paduka and Ardhkuwari, where devotees crawl through a narrow cave known as Garbh Joon.

After darshan, tradition holds that the yatra is complete only after visiting the Bhairon Nath temple, a further climb above the Bhawan, now also connected by a ropeway.

Highlights

  • Darshan of the three holy pindis in the cave
  • Trek of about 12-13 km from Katra
  • Ardhkuwari and Garbh Joon cave
  • Bhairon temple and ropeway
  • Helicopter from Katra to Sanjichhat

Things to do in Vaishno Devi

  1. Register for the yatra

    Yatra registration (RFID card) is mandatory. Register online on the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board website or at counters in Katra.

  2. Trek or use services

    Walk the main path or the newer Tarakote route; battery cars run from Ardhkuwari to Bhawan; ponies and palkis are available at fixed rates.

  3. Helicopter

    Helicopters fly from Katra to Sanjichhat, about 2.5 km from Bhawan. Book only on the Shrine Board's official website.

  4. Bhairon Nath temple

    Climb about 1.5 km from Bhawan or take the Bhawan-Bhairon ropeway to complete the yatra.

  5. Explore around Katra

    Visit Shiv Khori cave (about 80 km), Patnitop hill station and the Baba Dhansar shrine.

How to reach Vaishno Devi

By air

Jammu Airport (IXJ) is about 50 km from Katra, with flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Srinagar and others. Taxis and buses run to Katra.

By train

Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SVDK) station has direct trains, including Vande Bharat, from New Delhi and other cities.

By road

Katra is about 50 km from Jammu by road. J&K SRTC and private buses run frequently.

Getting around

Autos and taxis in Katra take you to the Banganga trek start point.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Shakti · Trikuta hills · Katra

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Devotees hold that the goddess manifests as three natural pindis, Mahakali, Mahalakshmi and Mahasaraswati, in the Bhawan cave.
  • Official statement The shrine is run by the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board, which sets registration, transport and accommodation rules.
  • Historical record The Bhawan is reached by a trek of roughly 12 to 14 km from Katra, a route used for generations.

Steps to follow

  1. Register for the yatra. The shrine board states pilgrims must register and carry an RFID card; do this online or at the Katra counter before you start.
  2. Reach Katra by train (Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra station) or by road from Jammu.
  3. Choose how to go up: walk, hire a pony, palki or battery car, or take the helicopter from Katra to Sanjichhat. Helicopter and online services are booked on the board's portal.
  4. Aim to start at night or before dawn in summer. Carry ID, a light layer, and water.
  5. After darshan, many continue to Bhairon Temple, a further climb or ropeway ride. Tradition says the visit is complete only after this.
  6. Book accommodation through the board for the Bhawan and Katra; private hotels in Katra fill at Navratri and holidays.

Make the trip count

  • Services and fees change often. Use the shrine board's own website for the current rates.
  • Do not buy yatra slips or helicopter tickets from third parties.
  • Winter weather can close paths. Check the board's notices.

Travel tips

  • Carry your yatra registration card - it is checked at Banganga.
  • Night trekking is common and the path is lit; carry a light jacket.
  • Leather items and bags are restricted inside the cave; use the lockers.
  • Book helicopter and Bhawan rooms only on the official Shrine Board site.
Etiquette. Maintain queue discipline at the Bhawan. Do not litter on the trek route.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Vaishno Devi trek?

About 12-13 km one way from Katra to Bhawan. Most people take 5-7 hours to climb.

Is yatra registration mandatory?

Yes, every pilgrim needs a valid yatra registration/RFID card.
