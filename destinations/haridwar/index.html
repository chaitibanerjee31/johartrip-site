About Haridwar

Haridwar - the 'gateway to Hari' - is where the Ganga leaves the Himalayas and enters the plains. It is one of the Sapta Puri and one of the four cities that host the Kumbh Mela, held here every twelve years with an Ardh Kumbh in between.

The centre of the city is Har Ki Pauri, where Lord Vishnu's footprint is believed to be imprinted. Every evening thousands gather for the Ganga Aarti as leaf-boats of flowers and diyas float downstream.

Ropeways lift pilgrims to the hilltop temples of Mansa Devi and Chandi Devi, and the city is also the traditional start of the Char Dham yatra and a centre for performing family rituals, with priests who keep genealogy records going back generations.

Highlights

  • Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri
  • Mansa Devi and Chandi Devi by ropeway
  • Kumbh Mela city
  • Family genealogy registers kept by priests
  • Start point of the Char Dham yatra

Things to do in Haridwar

  1. Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri

    Starts around sunset. Reach an hour early for a spot on the ghats or the clock tower side.

  2. Mansa Devi and Chandi Devi

    Take the ropeways (combo tickets available) to these Siddh Peeth temples on opposite hills.

  3. Holy dip

    Bathe at Har Ki Pauri using the chains and railings - the current is strong.

  4. Explore ashrams

    Visit Shantikunj, Patanjali Yogpeeth and Bharat Mata Mandir.

  5. Rajaji National Park

    Jeep safaris from the Chilla gate (Nov-June) for elephants and birds.

How to reach Haridwar

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (DED), about 35-40 km away.

By train

Haridwar Junction (HW) is connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and other cities, including Vande Bharat and Shatabdi trains from Delhi.

By road

About 220 km from Delhi via NH-334 with frequent buses.

Getting around

E-rickshaws and autos; walking around Har Ki Pauri.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Sapta Puri · Kumbh site · Gateway to Char Dham

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Haridwar is counted among the seven sacred cities (as Maya) in tradition.
  • Tradition It is one of four places that host the Kumbh Mela, along with Prayagraj, Nashik and Ujjain.
  • Tradition Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat for bathing and the evening Ganga Aarti.
  • Official statement It is the main rail gateway for the Uttarakhand Char Dham yatra.

Steps to follow

  1. Reach by train (Haridwar Junction) or from Dehradun airport (about 40 km).
  2. Attend the Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri at sunset. Arrive at least an hour early on weekends.
  3. Take the ropeway to Mansa Devi or Chandi Devi temples if the hill walk is too much.
  4. Register for Char Dham here if you continue; registration is mandatory.
  5. Add Rishikesh (about 25 km) for a second day.

Make the trip count

  • Do not leave valuables on the ghats while bathing.
  • Prices for 'puja packages' at the ghats vary hugely. Agree the amount first.

Confirmed on official pageRegistration

Char Dham yatra registration is compulsory (free)

The Uttarakhand portal says you must register yourself and your vehicle before the journey, download a Yatra Registration Letter with a QR code, and verify yourself at each shrine on arrival. It describes registration as free. Helicopter tickets are sold only through IRCTC.

Cost: Free, according to the official page.

How: Register on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in or the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app. Helpline 1364 (toll free) is listed on the page.

What we could not verify
  • Registration date windows and daily caps change each season.
  • Some third-party sites mention fees; the official page says free. Do not pay agents for registration.

Confirmed on official pageVehicle permit

Vehicle registration, Green Card and Trip Card for Char Dham routes

The official transport portal says commercial passenger vehicles (buses, minibuses, taxis) need a Green Card and Trip Card for the Char Dham shrines: ₹400 for up to 10 seats and ₹600 above 10 seats, plus a ₹50 user charge. Private vehicles are asked to register and pay a user charge; no Green Card is mentioned for them.

Cost: Commercial: ₹400 (up to 10 seats) or ₹600 (over 10 seats) plus ₹50 user charge. Private user-charge amount is not stated.

How: Register the vehicle at greencard.uk.gov.in (linked from transport.uk.gov.in).

What we could not verify
  • The private-vehicle user charge amount and Green Card validity are not stated on the page.
  • Some third-party sites say all passenger vehicles including tourists need a Green Card; the official page limits the Green Card to commercial vehicles.

Best time to visit

October to April. Avoid peak monsoon. Kanwar Yatra (July) is very crowded.

Month by month: January, February, March, April, October, November, December.

Festivals

Travel tips

  • Haridwar is a vegetarian, alcohol-free city.
  • Keep your belongings safe at crowded ghats.
  • Combine with Rishikesh, about 25 km away.
Etiquette. Dress modestly at ghats and temples; follow ghat safety rules.
