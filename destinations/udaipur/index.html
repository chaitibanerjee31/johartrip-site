About Udaipur

Udaipur was the capital of the Mewar kingdom and still shows it. The City Palace complex rises above Lake Pichola, with Jag Mandir and the Lake Palace on islands in the water. The old city around Gangaur Ghat and Jagdish Temple is a maze of lanes, shops and rooftop cafes.

The city is calm compared with Jaipur and Jodhpur, and easy to see on foot and by boat. Saheliyon ki Bari, Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace) and Fateh Sagar Lake fill out a two- or three-day visit.

Two big forts are within day-trip range: Kumbhalgarh, with its long wall, and Ranakpur, famous for its Jain temple. The Shilpgram crafts village hosts a large arts and crafts fair in late December.

Highlights

  • City Palace and Lake Pichola
  • Jagdish Temple
  • Saheliyon ki Bari garden
  • Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace for sunset
  • Shilpgram crafts village

Things to do in Udaipur

  1. Boat ride on Lake Pichola

    Go near sunset for views of the City Palace and island palaces.

  2. City Palace museum

    Allow two hours for courtyards, painted rooms and the crystal gallery.

  3. Old city walk

    Walk the lanes around Jagdish Temple and Gangaur Ghat for miniature paintings and spices.

  4. Sunset at Sajjangarh

    The hilltop palace has a wide view over the lakes.

  5. Day trip to Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur

    Combine the fort wall and the Jain temple in one long day.

How to reach Udaipur

By air

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) at Dabok is about 22 km from the city, with flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur and Ahmedabad.

By train

Udaipur City station has trains from Delhi, Jaipur and Ahmedabad.

By road

About 400 km from Jaipur and 260 km from Ahmedabad. Buses and taxis are frequent.

Getting around

Auto-rickshaws and app cabs work. The old city is best on foot.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Best time to visit

October to March. December and January are cool and busy; summer is hot.

Month by month: January, February, March, October, November, December.

Travel tips

  • Book lake-view hotels early for December.
  • Many palace areas charge separate entry fees.
  • Beware of touts selling boat rides; use official ticket counters.
Etiquette. Dress modestly in temples and cover your shoulders in the palace temples.

How many days do I need?

Two full days cover the main sights; add a third for Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur.
