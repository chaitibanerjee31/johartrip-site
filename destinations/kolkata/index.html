About Kolkata

Kolkata is a city of devotion as much as culture. Kalighat, one of the Shakti Peethas, gives the city its name. On the banks of the Hooghly, Dakshineswar Kali temple - where Sri Ramakrishna served as priest - faces Belur Math, the headquarters of the Ramakrishna Mission founded by Swami Vivekananda.

Every autumn Kolkata transforms during Durga Puja, inscribed by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage. Thousands of artistic pandals turn the whole city into an open-air gallery for five days.

Between temples, explore the Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, Kumartuli where idol-makers work, College Street book market, and a food scene of kathi rolls, phuchka, mishti doi and rosogolla.

The best way to feel the city is slowly: a morning ferry on the Hooghly, an afternoon in the bookshops of College Street and an evening of pandal-hopping or sweets in North Kolkata.

Things to do in Kolkata

  1. Kalighat temple

    Go early morning; ignore touts and pandas offering fast entry for large fees.

  2. Dakshineswar to Belur Math by ferry

    Visit Dakshineswar, then take the river ferry across to Belur Math for the evening aarti.

  3. Pandal hopping in Durga Puja

    Use the Metro and walk at night; North Kolkata has traditional bonedi bari pujas, South has themed pandals.

  4. Kumartuli

    Watch artisans sculpt clay idols, especially in the weeks before Durga Puja.

  5. Food walk

    Try phuchka, kathi rolls, Mughlai paratha, and sweets like rosogolla, sandesh and mishti doi.

How to reach Kolkata

By air

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU) connects to all major Indian cities and many international ones.

By train

Howrah (HWH), Sealdah (SDAH) and Shalimar stations connect to the whole country.

By road

National highways connect to Odisha, Bihar and North Bengal; Volvo buses run to Siliguri and Puri.

Getting around

Metro (including the under-river line to Howrah), yellow taxis, app cabs, trams on select routes and river ferries.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Travel tips

  • Use the Metro to beat traffic.
  • Book stays very early for Durga Puja week.
  • Gangasagar (for Makar Sankranti) is reachable from Kolkata.
Etiquette. Remove footwear at temples; photography is restricted inside Kalighat and Dakshineswar sanctums.

When is Durga Puja?

In Ashwin month, usually late September to October; the main days are Saptami to Dashami.
