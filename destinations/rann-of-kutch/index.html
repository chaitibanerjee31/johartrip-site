About Rann of Kutch

The White Rann is a huge salt marsh that floods in the monsoon and dries out by about October. From November it is firm enough to walk and drive across, and the horizon disappears into white. It is at its most striking at sunset and on clear full-moon nights.

Most visitors base themselves at Dhordo, the village next to the White Rann, or at Bhuj, the old walled town about 80 km away. The Rann Utsav, a cultural festival with a tent city, runs for several months over the winter. Dates and packages change every year, so check the official Gujarat tourism listing before you book.

Kutch is also one of India's richest craft regions. Villages around Bhuj, such as Bhujodi, Ajrakhpur and Nirona, are known for weaving, block printing, Rogan painting and leatherwork. Dholavira, a UNESCO-listed Harappan city, is a long day trip from the Rann.

Highlights

  • White Rann at sunset and moonlight
  • Rann Utsav tent city at Dhordo
  • Kala Dungar viewpoint
  • Bhuj: Aina Mahal and Prag Mahal
  • Craft villages: Bhujodi, Ajrakhpur, Nirona

Things to do in Rann of Kutch

  1. Watch sunset on the salt

    Go out onto the White Rann an hour before sunset and stay for the colour change.

  2. Stay at the tent city

    Rann Utsav tents are basic to luxury. Book early for December and for full-moon weekends.

  3. Visit Kala Dungar

    The highest point in Kutch has a wide view of the Rann on a clear day.

  4. Meet the craft families

    Visit weavers and block printers in the villages around Bhuj and buy directly.

  5. Day trip to Dholavira

    Explore the excavated Harappan city. It is a long drive, so start early.

How to reach Rann of Kutch

By air

Bhuj Airport (BHJ) has flights, mainly from Mumbai. Ahmedabad (AMD) is the main alternative.

By train

Bhuj railway station is the railhead, with direct trains from Ahmedabad, Mumbai and Delhi.

By road

Bhuj is about 330-340 km from Ahmedabad. Dhordo is about 80 km from Bhuj; most tent-city packages include transfers.

Getting around

Hire a taxi from Bhuj. There is no public transport to the Rann worth relying on.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Best time to visit

November to February. The tent city is usually open for much of this period, with the best weather in December and January. Summer is extremely hot and the Rann floods in the monsoon.

Month by month: January, February, November, December.

Festivals

Travel tips

  • Entry to the White Rann is controlled and a permit is required; ask your tent city or hotel how it is issued and check current rules.
  • Nights are cold in December and January. Carry a warm jacket.
  • Mobile networks are patchy at the Rann.
  • Do not walk far from the marked areas near the border.
Etiquette. The Rann is near the international border. Carry photo ID and follow security instructions. Respect local communities when photographing them.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Rann Utsav start?

It usually opens around the start of the cold season and runs for several months. Dates change every year, so confirm on the official tourism website.
