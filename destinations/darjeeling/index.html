About Darjeeling

Set at about 2,000 metres in the eastern Himalaya, Darjeeling is one of India's best-loved hill stations. On clear mornings, Kanchenjunga - the world's third-highest mountain - glows pink and gold above the clouds.

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, still runs steam and diesel 'toy train' rides, including the joy ride to Ghum and around the Batasia Loop.

Tea estates cover the slopes; a visit to a working garden and a cup of first-flush Darjeeling is a must. Monasteries such as Ghum and the Peace Pagoda add a spiritual note.

The town is spread along a ridge, so most visitors walk between the Mall, Chowrasta and the markets. Mornings are usually clearest for mountain views, while afternoons often bring mist rolling up from the valleys. Many travellers combine Darjeeling with Kalimpong, Mirik or Sikkim for a longer Himalayan holiday.

Things to do in Darjeeling

  1. Tiger Hill sunrise

    Leave around 4 am; entry is regulated and vehicles need permits, usually arranged by your taxi driver.

  2. Toy train joy ride

    Book on IRCTC in advance, especially in season.

  3. Tea estate visit

    Happy Valley and other estates offer tours and tastings.

  4. Padmaja Naidu Zoo and HMI

    See red pandas and snow leopards, and the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute museum.

  5. Mall Road and Chowrasta

    Stroll, shop for woollens and try momos and thukpa.

How to reach Darjeeling

By air

Bagdogra Airport (IXB), about 65-70 km away.

By train

New Jalpaiguri (NJP), about 65 km away, is the main railhead; the toy train runs from NJP to Darjeeling.

By road

Shared and private taxis from NJP/Siliguri take 3-4 hours.

Getting around

Walk around town; shared jeeps to nearby spots.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Travel tips

  • Carry warm layers year-round.
  • Combine with Kalimpong, Sikkim or Mirik.
  • Expect traffic jams on the hill road in peak season.
Etiquette. Be respectful in monasteries; walk clockwise around stupas.
