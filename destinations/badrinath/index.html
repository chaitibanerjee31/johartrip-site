About Badrinath

Badrinath is the only shrine that belongs to both the national Char Dham (with Dwarka, Puri and Rameswaram) and Uttarakhand's Chota Char Dham. The brightly painted temple of Lord Badrinarayan stands at about 3,100 metres beside the Alaknanda river, with the Neelkanth peak rising behind it.

Unlike Kedarnath, Badrinath can be reached by road all the way, which makes it accessible to elderly pilgrims. Before darshan, devotees bathe in the Tapt Kund, a natural hot spring right below the temple. Brahma Kapal, on the river bank, is where many perform shraddh rites for their ancestors.

A few kilometres beyond is Mana, the last village before the Tibet border, with Vyas Gufa, Ganesh Gufa and Bhim Pul, a natural rock bridge over the roaring Saraswati river.

Highlights

  • One of the four original Char Dham
  • Tapt Kund hot spring bath before darshan
  • Brahma Kapal for pind daan
  • Mana village, Vyas Gufa and Bhim Pul
  • Vasudhara Falls trek

Things to do in Badrinath

  1. Darshan and Maha Abhishek

    Special pujas like Maha Abhishek and Abhishek puja are held early in the morning and can be booked through the Badrinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee.

  2. Tapt Kund

    Bathe in the hot spring before entering the temple, as tradition recommends. Separate areas are available for women.

  3. Mana village

    About 3 km away. Visit Vyas Gufa, where Ved Vyas is believed to have composed the Mahabharata, Ganesh Gufa, and Bhim Pul over the Saraswati.

  4. Vasudhara Falls

    A moderate trek of about 5-6 km from Mana to a 120-metre waterfall. Carry water and start in the morning.

  5. Charan Paduka and Neelkanth views

    A short uphill walk to Charan Paduka gives sweeping views of Badrinath town and the Neelkanth peak at sunrise.

How to reach Badrinath

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (DED), about 315 km away.

By train

Yog Nagari Rishikesh and Haridwar, about 295-320 km away.

By road

Motorable all the way via Rishikesh, Devprayag, Rudraprayag, Joshimath (Jyotirmath). Buses and shared taxis run from Rishikesh and Haridwar during yatra season.

Getting around

The town is compact and walkable; shared jeeps go to Mana.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

For pilgrims: why it matters and how to go

Chota Char Dham · Char Dham (Badrinath) · Divya Desam

Why this place is important

  • Tradition Badrinath is among the 108 Divya Desams, Vishnu temples honoured in the Tamil Alvar hymns.
  • Tradition It is one of the four Char Dham pilgrimage sites that Shankaracharya tradition places at the four corners of India: Badrinath, Dwarka, Puri and Rameswaram.
  • Official statement It is also one of the four Chota Char Dham sites of Uttarakhand.
  • Tradition Tapt Kund hot spring at the temple and Mana village, the last Indian village on this road, are part of the usual visit.
  • Tradition The deity is worshipped for the six summer months. In winter the idol moves to Joshimath's Narsingh temple, according to temple custom.

Steps to follow

  1. Register on the Uttarakhand yatra portal first, as for all Char Dham sites.
  2. Reach by road from Rishikesh (about 300 km, 10 to 12 hours) with a stop at Joshimath or Pipalkoti. Badrinath has road access to the temple area.
  3. Bathe at Tapt Kund if you wish, then join the darshan queue. Early morning has the shortest line.
  4. Add Mana village, Vyas Gufa and the Saraswati river viewpoint, about 3 km away, on the same day.
  5. Combine with Kedarnath only if you can give it 7 to 10 days. Kedarnath to Badrinath is a long road transfer.

Make the trip count

  • Roads through Joshimath and the Chamoli district are landslide-prone. Check status each morning.
  • Mana village sits close to the border area. Carry photo ID; rules for visiting border areas can change.
  • Opening dates change each year with the calendar.

Confirmed on official pageRegistration

Char Dham yatra registration is compulsory (free)

The Uttarakhand portal says you must register yourself and your vehicle before the journey, download a Yatra Registration Letter with a QR code, and verify yourself at each shrine on arrival. It describes registration as free. Helicopter tickets are sold only through IRCTC.

Cost: Free, according to the official page.

How: Register on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in or the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app. Helpline 1364 (toll free) is listed on the page.

What we could not verify
  • Registration date windows and daily caps change each season.
  • Some third-party sites mention fees; the official page says free. Do not pay agents for registration.

Confirmed on official pageVehicle permit

Vehicle registration, Green Card and Trip Card for Char Dham routes

The official transport portal says commercial passenger vehicles (buses, minibuses, taxis) need a Green Card and Trip Card for the Char Dham shrines: ₹400 for up to 10 seats and ₹600 above 10 seats, plus a ₹50 user charge. Private vehicles are asked to register and pay a user charge; no Green Card is mentioned for them.

Cost: Commercial: ₹400 (up to 10 seats) or ₹600 (over 10 seats) plus ₹50 user charge. Private user-charge amount is not stated.

How: Register the vehicle at greencard.uk.gov.in (linked from transport.uk.gov.in).

What we could not verify
  • The private-vehicle user charge amount and Green Card validity are not stated on the page.
  • Some third-party sites say all passenger vehicles including tourists need a Green Card; the official page limits the Green Card to commercial vehicles.

Travel tips

  • Char Dham registration is mandatory.
  • Night driving in the hills is not permitted - plan stays in Joshimath or Pipalkoti.
  • Carry warm clothes; nights are cold even in June.
  • Keep buffer days in case roads close.
Etiquette. Traditional clothes are preferred inside the temple. Follow the queue system and priests' instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can elderly people visit Badrinath?

Yes. The road goes up to the town and the temple is a short walk from the parking area.

What is the difference between Char Dham and Chota Char Dham?

Char Dham are Badrinath, Dwarka, Puri and Rameswaram across India. Chota Char Dham are Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath in Uttarakhand.
