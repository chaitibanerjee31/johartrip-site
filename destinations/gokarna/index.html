About Gokarna

Gokarna has two sides: a pilgrim town around the Mahabaleshwar Temple and a line of beaches just south. Om Beach, Kudle, Half Moon and Paradise Beach are linked by coastal trails and boats.

The beaches have simple shacks and guesthouses, with a laid-back feel. December is the busiest month, but crowds are smaller than in Goa. Evenings are for sunsets and small cafes.

Currents can be strong, so swim only where locals do and where lifeguards are posted. Carry cash, as ATMs are limited near the beaches.

Highlights

  • Om Beach
  • Kudle Beach
  • Half Moon and Paradise beaches
  • Mahabaleshwar Temple
  • Coastal trek between beaches

Things to do in Gokarna

  1. Beach-hop on foot

    Walk the cliff trails between Kudle, Om and Half Moon.

  2. Sunset at Om Beach

    One of the best spots on the coast.

  3. Temple visit

    See the Mahabaleshwar Temple in the old town.

  4. Boat trips

    Dolphin-spotting trips run from some beaches.

  5. Try local seafood

    Beach shacks serve fresh fish and Konkani food.

How to reach Gokarna

By air

Goa (Dabolim/Mopa) is about 140-150 km; Mangaluru is about 230 km.

By train

Gokarna Road station is about 9 km from town; Ankola is nearby.

By road

About 150 km from Panaji and 450 km from Bengaluru.

Getting around

Autos, scooters and boats.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Gokarna or Goa?
