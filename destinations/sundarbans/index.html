About Sundarbans

The Indian Sundarbans lies in the delta of the Ganga, Brahmaputra and Meghna. Tides rise and fall twice a day, flooding the mangroves and creeks. The forest is home to the Royal Bengal tiger, which swims between the islands.

Most visitors travel from Kolkata by road to jetties such as Godkhali or Sonakhali, then board boats. Day tours exist, but overnight trips give more time. Permits are arranged by registered operators.

Tigers are elusive, so enjoy the birds, crocodiles, deer and the mangrove itself. November to February is the best season; the monsoon and cyclone season are best avoided.

Things to do in Sundarbans

  1. Boat safari

    Spend hours on the water; sightings are never guaranteed.

  2. Watchtowers

    Sit at waterholes for deer and birds.

  3. Village visit

    See life on Gosaba island.

  4. Mangrove walk

    Visit canopy walks such as Dobanki.

  5. Overnight cruise

    Choose a licensed operator with safety gear.

How to reach Sundarbans

By air

Kolkata (CCU) is the nearest airport.

By train

Canning or Sonakhali stations connect to Kolkata's suburban network.

By road

About 100 km from Kolkata to Godkhali, 3-4 hours.

Getting around

Boats operate from jetties.

Book trains on IRCTC, the official site.

Best time to visit

November to February is dry and comfortable. Avoid the monsoon and cyclone season (June-September).

Month by month: January, February, November, December.

Festivals

Bonbibi puja (January/February)

Travel tips

  • Use a licensed operator; permits are required.
  • Mobile signal is patchy.
  • Never step off the boat in tiger zones.
Etiquette. Keep noise and litter down. Respect restricted areas and the rules of the forest department.
