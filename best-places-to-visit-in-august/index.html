Best places to visit in India in August

Plan your August trip with weather-matched pilgrimage and holiday guides.

August remains wet in most regions, so keep buffer days for delays. Janmashtami and Raksha Bandhan bring crowds to Krishna towns and temples.

Our guides rate 5 places as at their best in August: Hanle and Changthang homestays, Markha Valley trek homestays, Nubra Valley and Turtuk homestays, Sham Valley homestays: Alchi, Likir and Ule, Spiti Valley homestays.

Festivals this month

  • Rameswaram: Thirukalyanam (July-Aug)

Less ideal in August

  • Badrinath: May-June and September-October. Monsoon landslides make July-August risky. Closed in winter (around November to April).
  • Dooars homestays: October to March for wildlife and mild weather. Summer is hot and humid; parks may close in the monsoon.
  • Goa: November to February. Monsoon (June-Sept) is lush and cheap but beaches are rough.
  • Haridwar: October to April. Avoid peak monsoon. Kanwar Yatra (July) is very crowded.
  • Jibhi and Tirthan Valley homestays: March to June and September to November. Winter has snow at higher points; monsoon brings landslide risk.
  • Kedarnath: May-June and mid-September to October. Avoid July-August monsoon, when landslides frequently block roads. The temple is closed from November to April.
  • Lava, Kolakham and Rishyap homestays: March to May and October to December for clear mountain views. Monsoon (June to September) is green and quiet but has landslide risk and leeches.
  • Parvati Valley homestays: Kasol, Tosh and Kalga: March to June and September to November; winters are cold and monsoons bring landslides.
  • Rishikesh: September to November and February to May. Rafting is closed in the monsoon.
  • Sandakphu and Singalila trail stays: April to May and October to November for clear views. Winter is very cold with snow; monsoon is wet and views are poor.
  • Vaishno Devi : March to June and September to October. Navratri is auspicious but crowded. Winter (Dec-Jan) brings snow and cold; monsoon can cause slippery paths.
  • Varanasi : October to March is pleasant. Summers (April-June) are very hot; during monsoon the river rises and some ghats and boat services close.
