Best places to visit in India in October

Plan your October trip with weather-matched pilgrimage and holiday guides.

October is the start of the best travel season: clear skies, cooler days and the big festivals, including Navratri, Dussehra and Durga Puja. Popular places are crowded and prices rise.

Our guides rate 27 places as at their best in October: Ayodhya, Badrinath, Darjeeling, Dooars homestays, Dwarka, Hanle and Changthang homestays, Haridwar, Jaipur, Jibhi and Tirthan Valley homestays, Kamakhya, Kedarnath, Kinnaur homestays: Sangla, Chitkul and Kalpa, Kolkata, Lava, Kolakham and Rishyap homestays, Parvati Valley homestays: Kasol, Tosh and Kalga, Puri, Rameswaram, Rishikesh, Sham Valley homestays: Alchi, Likir and Ule, Sandakphu and Singalila trail stays, Sittong homestays, Somnath, Spiti Valley homestays, Tirupati & Tirumala, Ujjain, Vaishno Devi, Varanasi.
