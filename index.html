Questions travellers ask

Is JoharTrip free to use?

Yes. All guides and the trip planner are free and need no sign-up. JoharTrip earns from clearly labelled partner links and paid business listings.

What does JoharTrip cover?

Travel and pilgrimage guides for India: temples and yatra routes such as Char Dham, the 12 Jyotirlingas and Shakti Peethas, plus cities, hills, beaches and village homestays. Each guide covers how to reach, the best months, things to do, and permits and rules.

How do you check the information?

Every guide shows the date it was last checked. Permits and rules are labelled by how sure we are and list their sources. Timings, fees and opening dates change, so confirm on official websites before you travel.

Can I book hotels or darshan on JoharTrip?

No. JoharTrip does not take bookings or payments from travellers. We link to official temple and government portals for darshan and registration, and to partner sites for stays and transport.

I run a homestay, hotel or tour business. Can I be listed?

Yes. Use the List your business page to add a labelled listing on the guide for your place.
